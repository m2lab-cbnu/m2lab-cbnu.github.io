    The holo room: our lab in 3D

    This is our real lab, rebuilt in 3D, and its instruments run real physics in your browser. Walk around, open a station, change the film or the field, and see what happens. The door takes you back to the virtual building.

    • FMRFMR setup: resonance lines, field–frequency maps, damping and the angle dependence.
    • kBlackboard: spin-wave dispersion, antennas and isofrequency curves.
    • PCSimulation PC: a micromagnetic simulator that runs on your graphics card, in the spirit of mumax3.
    • BLSBLS setup: Brillouin light scattering, from spectra and spatial maps to parametric pumping, magnon condensation and squeezing.

    Drag to look around, W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, click an instrument to use it, and Esc to step back. On a phone, tap the floor to walk. On a slow computer you can use the instruments without the 3D room.
