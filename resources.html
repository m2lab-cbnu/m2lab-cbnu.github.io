Resources

Interactive demos, short physics notes and small tools from M² Lab, written for students and anyone curious about physics.

Interactive demos

  • Virtual lab

    A three-floor pixel-art lab you can walk around, with experiments that run real physics: pendulums, lenses and collisions, a magnet falling through a copper pipe, the double slit, and our FMR, spin-wave, micromagnetic and Brillouin light scattering tools. Our real lab is in there too, in 3D, and you can switch the whole thing to Korean.

    Open the virtual lab

  • Magnetic field calculator

    Work out the B and H fields around a conductor with a rectangular cross-section, using Biot–Savart summed over many thin filaments.

    Open calculator

  • Sound-wave synthesizer

    Build a sound out of sine waves and hear it live, with a scope view and a spectrum.

    Open demo

  • Coupled oscillators

    Watch energy slosh back and forth between coupled oscillators, and find their normal modes.

    Open demo
